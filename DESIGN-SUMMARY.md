# Design summary

- **Concept:** Offshore operations in the current Stealth Agents marketing family while keeping Offshore Resourcing primary: white compact navigation, dark photographic first folds, cyan-highlighted display type, green operational checks, bright blue actions, immediate white proof bands, long light editorial sections, pale-blue support surfaces, and full-width navy conversion blocks.
- **Reference use:** The current Stealth Agents site supplies composition and visual grammar. Approved Stealth Agents attribution assets and the official Bacolod team photograph are reused only in their documented identity-bearing roles; no Stealth Agents copy, unsupported claims, testimonials beyond the approved review source, or unapproved proprietary media is copied.
- **Typography:** Locally bundled Montserrat variable, weights 400 through 800.
- **Palette:** Near-black `#020813`, deep navy `#101827`, white, cool gray `#f7f9fc`, Offshore Resourcing blue `#0aa9e8`, light cyan `#72d3fb`, and restrained green status cues.
- **Layout:** 1216px maximum shell, 24px desktop gutters, 16px mobile gutters, roughly 92px desktop section rhythm, and purpose-specific page compositions.
- **Shape language:** 8px controls, 12px media, 16px cards, 20px conversion panels, subtle borders, and restrained shadows.
- **First folds:** Dark photographic compositions with a rounded trust label, oversized white and cyan display type, operational checks, strong blue action, approved documentary imagery, and useful operating context.
- **Navigation:** Sticky white header with Home, Services, and Contact Us; subsidiary identity stays primary; StealthAgents attribution remains visible on desktop and in the footer; mobile uses a compact accessible menu.
- **Content families:** Commercial, service detail, editorial, research, contact, scheduler, legal, confirmation, 404, header, and footer share the same tokens while retaining layouts suited to their purpose.
- **Motion:** Fast interface transitions, a pausable review marquee, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
- **Primary implementation:** `app/reference-system.css`, loaded after legacy CSS as the authoritative visual layer. Route-owned styling remains in the contact and top-30 comparison CSS modules.
