# Remove offshore access when work ends

An offboarding check that closes accounts and files.

## Why this control matters

The access removal control starts with a written purpose. Keep the source, owner, and next date visible before the item moves.

## What to check first

Check one concrete case: compare the access removal record with the approved source and label the mismatch.

## Handle the exceptions

Log the exception with the date and owner. Record whether the fix belongs in the source, the checklist, or the review step.

## Review the evidence

Review the access removal queue weekly and close only when the source, approval, and destination evidence agree.
