# Track compliance document expiry

A register for document dates and renewal owners.

## Why this control matters

The compliance documents control starts with a written purpose. Keep the source, owner, and next date visible before the item moves.

## What to check first

Check one concrete case: compare the compliance documents record with the approved source and label the mismatch.

## Handle the exceptions

Log the exception with the date and owner. Record whether the fix belongs in the source, the checklist, or the review step.

## Review the evidence

Review the compliance documents queue weekly and close only when the source, approval, and destination evidence agree.
