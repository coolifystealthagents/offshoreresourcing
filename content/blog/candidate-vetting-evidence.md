# Check candidate vetting evidence before assignment

A completeness check for identity, references, and role evidence.

## Why this control matters

The candidate vetting control starts with a written purpose. Keep the source, owner, and next date visible before the item moves.

## What to check first

Check one concrete case: compare the candidate vetting record with the approved source and label the mismatch.

## Handle the exceptions

Log the exception with the date and owner. Record whether the fix belongs in the source, the checklist, or the review step.

## Review the evidence

Review the candidate vetting queue weekly and close only when the source, approval, and destination evidence agree.
