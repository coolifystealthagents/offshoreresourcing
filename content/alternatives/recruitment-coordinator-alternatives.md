## Key takeaways

- Use a coordinator when scheduling, records, and handoffs are recurring.
- Use a recruiter when sourcing and candidate assessment are the primary need.
- Automation helps with stable scheduling rules but cannot own hiring decisions.

## Options compared

| Option | Best for | Watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dedicated coordinator | Recurring multi-manager queue | Needs clear approval rules |
| Shared assistant | Light scheduling volume | Competing priorities |
| Recruiter | Search and qualification | Higher cost for admin-only work |
| Agency | Flexible hiring campaigns | Continuity and data handoff |
| Automation | Repeatable calendar rules | Exceptions and candidate care |

## Decision checklist

- How many interviews and handoffs occur each week?
- Who decides candidate progression?
- Which systems contain personal information?
- What response window and time-zone overlap are required?
