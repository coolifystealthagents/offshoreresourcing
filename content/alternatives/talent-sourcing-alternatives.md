## Key takeaways

- A narrow search brief matters more than a large candidate list.
- Software expands reach but does not validate fit or own outreach policy.
- Evaluate record quality, duplicate handling, and evidence for each match.

## Options compared

| Option | Useful when | Main constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-house sourcer | Hiring is continuous and strategic | Fixed internal capacity |
| Offshore specialist | Search lanes are documented | Manager review remains essential |
| Full-cycle recruiter | One person owns more of the funnel | Admin work may be costly |
| Agency | Fast access to an external network | Fees and handoff visibility |
| Sourcing software | Internal team can operate the tool | Data and outreach governance |

## How to choose

Provide every option with the same role brief and a fictional sourcing exercise. Score fit evidence, record completeness, duplicate handling, source compliance, and the clarity of the manager handoff.
