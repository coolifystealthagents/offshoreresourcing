## Key takeaways

- Planning support organizes approved data; managers still decide headcount and budget.
- Consultants fit redesign work, while support specialists fit recurring record preparation.
- Software only helps when source records and ownership are reliable.

## Options compared

| Option | Best fit | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internal operations analyst | Sensitive, strategic planning | Internal capacity |
| Offshore support specialist | Recurring data and draft views | Needs manager approval |
| Consultant | One-time model redesign | Less daily continuity |
| Staffing agency | Hiring execution | May not own planning records |
| Workforce platform | Stable data and mature process | Implementation and governance |

## Decision boundaries

Keep headcount, overtime, leave, policy, and budget decisions with authorized managers. The support role can combine records, flag gaps, prepare options, and update approved changes.
