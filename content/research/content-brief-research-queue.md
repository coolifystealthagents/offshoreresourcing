## Key stats

- Queue priority is stronger when it combines value and readiness.
- A topic without defensible sources is not ready for drafting.
- WIP limits protect reviewers from a growing unfinished queue.

## Key takeaways

- Rank by audience need, evidence confidence, and effort.
- Keep blocked topics visible with a named unblock action.
- Cap active briefs to the team’s review capacity.

## Queue fields

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | Defines reader outcome |
| Evidence | Shows source readiness |
| Effort | Supports realistic scheduling |
| Status | Makes WIP visible |

The [research brief gates](/research/research-brief-quality-gates) define readiness; the [editorial SLA](/research/offshore-editorial-review-sla) protects the review lane.
