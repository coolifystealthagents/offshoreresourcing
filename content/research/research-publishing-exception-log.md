## Key stats

- Four fields make an exception actionable: issue, evidence, owner, and unblock action.
- A shortfall is a control signal, not a successful batch.
- NIST CSF 2.0 is designed to help organizations manage cybersecurity risk through structured outcomes.

## Key takeaways

- Log exceptions before changing scope.
- Name the owner who can unblock the item.
- Close the exception only after revalidation.

## Exception fields

| Field | Required content | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issue | Exact failed check | Missing primary source |
| Evidence | File, command, or URL | Claim row 12 |
| Owner | Person or role | Research editor |
| Unblock | Next concrete action | Obtain source table |

## Operating method

Use the [content risk register](/research/content-risk-register) and keep exceptions separate from accepted articles. If a topic cannot meet the Research standard, replace it or leave the run nonterminal with the precise deficit. Never convert an exception into filler content.
