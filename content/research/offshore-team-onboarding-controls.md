## Key stats

- Least-privilege access limits the impact of mistakes and compromised accounts.
- Early sample review catches instruction gaps before scope expands.
- Named approval owners prevent silent decision drift.

## Key takeaways

- Grant only the access needed for the first task.
- Review real samples during the first week.
- Expand scope only after accuracy and escalation behavior are stable.

## First-month sequence

| Period | Scope | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Observe, train, and complete samples | Manager-approved examples |
| Week 2 | Small live queue | Daily review and clean handoff |
| Weeks 3–4 | Controlled expansion | Stable quality and access review |
