## Key stats

- A review SLA needs a start event, stop event, and owner.
- Fast review is not successful if material defects escape the gate.
- Escalation thresholds should be defined before a deadline is missed.

## Key takeaways

- Separate response time from resolution time.
- Record blocked states and the evidence needed to unblock them.
- Measure rework after review, not just review speed.

## SLA design table

| Element | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Complete draft enters review queue |
| Response | Reviewer acknowledges within window |
| Resolution | Decision and comments are recorded |
| Escalation | Owner is notified after threshold |
