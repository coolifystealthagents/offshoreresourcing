## Key stats
- A refresh trigger should point to a specific change, not a vague sense of age.
- Evidence, intent, accessibility, and metadata can age at different rates.
- Recording the trigger makes refresh priority explainable.
## Key takeaways
- Refresh central facts before cosmetic copy.
- Compare current intent with the original brief.
- Close or replace pages that no longer serve a clear purpose.
## Trigger matrix
Use the [content refresh decision rules](/research/content-refresh-decision-rules) to route updates.
| Trigger | First check | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New data | Numbers and dates | Update claims |
| Changed guidance | Method and links | Rebrief |
| Intent shift | Reader question | Rescope |
| Broken source | Evidence chain | Replace |
## Methodology and limitations
This framework synthesizes public research, search, statistics, quality, privacy, and accessibility guidance. It cannot determine commercial value or audience preference without local evidence.

## FAQs
### Should every old article be refreshed?
No. Refresh when evidence, intent, usefulness, or technical integrity warrants the work.
### What is the first post-refresh check?
Revalidate claims, links, dates, metadata, and the article's stated purpose.
