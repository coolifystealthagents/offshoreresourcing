## Key stats

- Source count is a quality signal only when sources are relevant, traceable, and independently useful.
- Budgeting should reserve time for verification and synthesis, not only discovery.
- A source register reduces repeated browsing across writers and reviewers.

## Key takeaways

- Set a minimum evidence plan per article before research begins.
- Spend extra time where claims affect cost, compliance, or hiring decisions.
- Stop discovery when the evidence answers the brief and new sources add little.

## Budget model

| Stage | Output | Stop signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Candidate source list | Scope covered |
| Verification | Checked source register | Claims traceable |
| Synthesis | Findings and limits | Pattern is clear |
| Review | Corrections and notes | Gate passes |
