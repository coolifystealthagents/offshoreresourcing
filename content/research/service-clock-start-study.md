## Research question and scope

In offshore recruitment and managed staffing: Two reviewers classify the same cases before discussion. Compare first-pass judgments and group differences by evidence, definition, authority, or calculation.

At Offshore Resourcing: Agreement is not accuracy. Two reviewers can agree on the same incomplete file. Follow agreement with a completeness check.

## Limitations and error

In the offshore recruitment and managed staffing lane: A system timestamp may record an open event rather than a complete request. A pause may be applied verbally and never logged. A holiday calendar can hide a late start.
