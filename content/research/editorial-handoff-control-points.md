## Key stats

- Five handoffs are common: intake, research, drafting, review, and release.
- A handoff without an owner creates an unmeasured queue.
- Google describes content quality through helpfulness, reliability, and people-first purpose.

## Key takeaways

- Pass a compact evidence packet with every handoff.
- Set an acceptance rule before work changes hands.
- Return incomplete work to the current owner with one explicit next action.

## Control points

| Handoff | Required packet | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Audience and intent | Brief approved |
| Research | Source register and claims | Evidence accepted |
| Draft | Article and internal links | Structure complete |
| Review | Defect list and decisions | Blockers closed |

## Operating method

Use the [remote writer handoff controls](/research/remote-writer-handoff-controls) with the [remote editorial review queue](/research/remote-editorial-review-queue). The receiving owner should acknowledge the packet, not merely receive a notification. Escalate after one missed acceptance window.
