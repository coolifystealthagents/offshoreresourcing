## Key stats

- Access reviews should cover who has access, why they need it, and when it was last checked.
- Least privilege reduces unnecessary exposure while preserving the work needed for a role.
- Joiner, mover, and leaver events are stronger triggers than a calendar-only review.

## Key takeaways

- Maintain a role-to-resource matrix.
- Remove stale access promptly and record exceptions.
- Keep content permissions separate from deployment permissions.

## Review matrix

| Resource | Default role | Review trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Briefs | Research and editorial | Role change |
| Source register | Research and editor | Quarterly review |
| Drafts | Writer and editor | Assignment close |
| Production branch | Approved publisher | Release change |
