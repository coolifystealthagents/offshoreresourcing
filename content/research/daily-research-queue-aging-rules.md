## Key stats

- Three aging states are sufficient for a daily queue: current, review-needed, and stale.
- A queue item without a next action is not active work.
- Source dates should be visible because evidence ages at different rates.

## Key takeaways

- Age the brief and the sources independently.
- Reconfirm intent when a topic changes materially.
- Retire stale work instead of carrying it indefinitely.

## Aging rules

| State | Signal | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current | Owner and evidence are current | Produce |
| Review-needed | Source or intent needs check | Rebrief |
| Stale | No owner or defensible evidence | Retire or replace |

## Operating method

Apply the [research intake prioritization framework](/research/research-intake-prioritization-framework), then compare candidates against existing Research slugs. Google advises creating content for an intended audience and purpose, so a stale topic should be requalified rather than mechanically published.
