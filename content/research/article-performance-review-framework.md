## Key stats

- A metric needs a defined owner, denominator, period, and decision use.
- Traffic alone cannot establish whether an article served its intent.
- Review should separate content quality from distribution effects.

## Key takeaways

- Combine reader, quality, and maintenance signals.
- Compare like periods and document data limitations.
- Turn every review into keep, update, test, or retire action.

## Review table

| Signal | Decision it informs |
| --- | --- |
| Search impressions | Visibility opportunity |
| Engagement | Reader usefulness question |
| Rework | Quality or brief issue |
| Source age | Maintenance priority |
