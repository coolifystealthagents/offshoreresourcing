## Key stats

- Continuity depends on documented ownership and recoverable work products.
- Critical publishing steps should have a tested fallback owner.
- A plan is incomplete until recovery evidence is recorded.

## Key takeaways

- Identify essential workflows, dependencies, and minimum service levels.
- Keep current briefs, sources, and drafts in the canonical workspace.
- Test handoff and recovery with a small realistic exercise.

## Continuity table

| Risk | Prepared control |
| --- | --- |
| Absence | Named backup owner |
| Tool outage | Exported work and alternate channel |
| Source change | Verification date and notes |
| Approval delay | Escalation route and buffer |

The [secure access model](/research/secure-remote-content-access) protects recovery materials; the [calendar controls](/research/remote-content-calendar-controls) make buffers visible.
