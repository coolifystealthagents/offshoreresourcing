## Key stats
- Central unsupported claims require escalation before publication.
- Severity is determined by reader impact, not by word count.
- A written disposition is part of the evidence record.
## Key takeaways
- Triage claims by impact and uncertainty.
- Ask the source owner a narrow question.
- Preserve unresolved issues in the exception log.
## Escalation path
Use the [content QA severity matrix](/research/content-qa-severity-matrix) to route factual, methodological, access, and presentation issues.
| Severity | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Critical | Stop and obtain evidence |
| High | Reviewer decision before release |
| Medium | Correct and recheck |
| Low | Log for next pass |
## Methodology and limitations
This path uses public research, statistics, quality, security, privacy, and accessibility principles. It is not a substitute for legal or regulated-industry review.

## FAQs
### What counts as central?
A claim is central when removing or changing it alters the article's main answer or recommendation.
### Can an editor waive a check?
Only with a documented reason, owner, and follow-up date.
