## Key stats

- Rework is actionable when its cause is recorded rather than treated as a generic failure.
- The four useful classes are scope, evidence, structure, and presentation.
- Trend counts should inform process changes, not blame individuals.

## Key takeaways

- Fix upstream ambiguity before increasing downstream review.
- Preserve examples of recurring defects in the playbook.
- Close each material correction with a named prevention action.

## Rework taxonomy

| Class | Typical defect | Prevention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Wrong audience or intent | Brief acceptance |
| Evidence | Unsupported claim | Claim register |
| Structure | Missing required section | Article outline |
| Presentation | Link or table issue | Final QA |
