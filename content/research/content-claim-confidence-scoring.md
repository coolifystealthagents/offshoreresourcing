## Key stats

- A claim register should record the claim, source, date, confidence, and reviewer.
- Triangulation is strongest when sources are independent and answer the same question.
- Confidence scores guide review effort; they do not replace editorial judgment.

## Key takeaways

- Score source authority, directness, recency, and agreement separately.
- Keep recommendations visibly distinct from sourced facts.
- Reopen high-impact claims when their source or context changes.

## Scoring model

| Dimension | Low signal | Strong signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authority | Unclear publisher | Official or primary source |
| Directness | Indirect mention | Direct measurement |
| Recency | Undated | Dated and current |
| Agreement | Contradicted | Independently corroborated |
