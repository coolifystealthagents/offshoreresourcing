## Key stats

- The recommended register has 8 fields: claim, source, publisher, date, method, excerpt, owner, and review status.
- Research with one source per claim is easier to audit than research stored in chat threads.
- Source freshness is a decision field, not a decorative timestamp.

## Key takeaways

- Separate source discovery from source acceptance.
- Capture the exact claim supported by each source.
- Flag opinion, secondary reporting, and stale data explicitly.

## Register design

| Field | Why it matters | Example status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claim | Narrows what is being asserted | Draft |
| URL and publisher | Enables retrieval | Accepted |
| Date and method | Establishes recency and provenance | Verified |
| Owner and review date | Creates accountability | Scheduled |

## Operating method

Start with the [source verification workflow](/research/source-verification-workflow), then use [research source triangulation](/research/research-source-triangulation) to test important claims across independent sources. Keep the register with the brief so a future editor can refresh the article without reconstructing the research.
