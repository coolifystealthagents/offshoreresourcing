## Key stats

- Services represent a large share of Philippine employment.
- National indicators describe a market, not the ability of an individual applicant.
- A work sample remains the clearest role-level check.

## Key takeaways

- Use national data to frame sourcing, never as a hiring promise.
- Validate writing, systems, judgment, and handoffs against the actual role.
- Record the indicator year, unit, and source.

## What the data can tell you

Labor, education, connectivity, and industry data can help a buyer understand the operating environment. It cannot replace an approved role brief or a consistent candidate assessment.
