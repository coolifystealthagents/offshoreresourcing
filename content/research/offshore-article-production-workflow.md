## Key stats

- A documented workflow turns recurring publishing into repeatable handoffs.
- Editorial checklists make omissions visible before publication.
- Source notes preserve the path from a claim to its evidence.

## Key takeaways

- Define the brief before assigning research or writing.
- Use a named review gate for facts, links, and metadata.
- Record exceptions instead of silently moving incomplete work forward.

## Workflow control table

| Stage | Required output | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan | Intent, audience, and sources | Approved brief |
| Draft | Complete article and citations | Writer checklist |
| Review | Fact, link, and structure checks | Reviewer sign-off |
| Publish | File, image, and metadata | Preflight record |
