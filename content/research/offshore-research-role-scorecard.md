## Key stats

- A useful scorecard measures observable work, not personality or vague effort.
- Research quality combines source judgment, traceability, writing clarity, and reliable handoff.
- A role should have an escalation route for uncertainty and access limits.

## Key takeaways

- Define evidence quality before assigning production volume.
- Use examples of acceptable and unacceptable claim notes.
- Review scorecards after the first completed cycle.

## Scorecard dimensions

| Dimension | Observable signal | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sources | Complete source register | Can a claim be retraced? |
| Reasoning | Limits are stated | Are findings qualified? |
| Delivery | Handoff is complete | Can the next role act? |
| Judgment | Risks are escalated | Were uncertainties surfaced? |
