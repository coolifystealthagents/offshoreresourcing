## Key stats
- Metadata errors can undermine otherwise sound research.
- Title, description, canonical path, dates, and image should agree with the article.
- A checklist catches mechanical defects before release.
## Key takeaways
- Check metadata after content edits.
- Keep dates honest and source-backed.
- Validate links and structured fields together.
## Checklist
Run the [research metadata completeness check](/research/research-metadata-completeness-check) after fact review.
| Field | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| Title | Does it match reader intent? |
| Description | Does it describe the article? |
| Dates | Are they accurate and current? |
| Image | Does the asset exist and fit? |
## Methodology and limitations
This checklist synthesizes public search, accessibility, quality, research, and plain-language guidance. It cannot replace a site-specific schema or build test.

## FAQs
### When should metadata be reviewed?
Review it at brief approval, after substantial edits, and immediately before publication.
### What is a canonical path?
It is the preferred stable URL for the article and should match the site's routing rules.
