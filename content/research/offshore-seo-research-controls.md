## Key stats

- Search terms are signals of intent, not substitutes for reader research.
- Unique coverage requires checking existing titles, slugs, and angles.
- Internal links work best when they answer a connected next question.

## Key takeaways

- Define the query and reader task before drafting.
- Check the existing inventory for overlap and cannibalization.
- Make each link useful in context and verify its destination.

## Preflight table

| Check | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | One-sentence reader task |
| Uniqueness | Inventory and slug check |
| Evidence | Source-to-claim notes |
| Links | Two relevant checked destinations |
