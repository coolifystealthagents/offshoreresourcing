## Key stats

- Authority, relevance, recency, and claim fit are separate checks.
- A source list without claim mapping makes review slower.
- Verification dates matter when policies, rates, or datasets change.

## Key takeaways

- Prefer primary and official sources for material claims.
- Save the exact source URL beside the supported statement.
- Recheck volatile evidence immediately before publication.

## Verification matrix

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Authority | Publisher is identifiable and appropriate |
| Relevance | Source addresses the article question |
| Date | Publication or update date is recorded |
| Traceability | Reader can find the supporting passage |
