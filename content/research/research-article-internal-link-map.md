## Key stats

- Three link roles keep a Research cluster coherent: prerequisite, adjacent method, and next action.
- Each article should expose at least two useful contextual internal links.
- Link relevance matters more than link volume.

## Key takeaways

- Map links to reader progression, not keyword repetition.
- Use descriptive labels that explain the destination.
- Check every target slug during validation.

## Link map

| Role | Reader need | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prerequisite | Understand the control | Research brief gates |
| Adjacent method | Apply the control | Source verification |
| Next action | Continue the workflow | QA checklist |

## Operating method

Create the map while writing the brief. Link the [remote content calendar controls](/research/remote-content-calendar-controls) to the [research brief quality gates](/research/research-brief-quality-gates), then confirm target pages exist. Google’s people-first guidance supports links that help readers complete their task.
