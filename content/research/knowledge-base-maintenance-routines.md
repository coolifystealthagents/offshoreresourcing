## Key stats

- Published content accumulates maintenance obligations as sources change.
- A review date is different from a publication date.
- Ownership and disposition make stale-content queues actionable.

## Key takeaways

- Classify articles as keep, update, consolidate, or retire.
- Recheck high-impact claims and links on a defined cadence.
- Preserve change notes so reviewers know what moved.

## Maintenance queue

| Trigger | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Source updated | Recheck affected claims |
| Link fails | Replace or remove after review |
| Intent changes | Rebrief and assess overlap |
| Owner leaves | Assign a new accountable owner |

Use the [source verification workflow](/research/source-verification-workflow) for claims and the [calendar controls](/research/remote-content-calendar-controls) for due dates.
