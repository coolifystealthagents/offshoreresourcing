## Key stats

- Capacity is usable time after meetings, QA, handoffs, and planned leave are removed.
- A buffer absorbs variation; it is not permission to lower quality gates.
- APQC process frameworks support measuring work by process and performance outcome.

## Key takeaways

- Plan by review hours as well as article count.
- Reserve named coverage for urgent factual corrections.
- Recalculate the buffer after two delivery cycles.

## Capacity table

| Work item | Planned input | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
