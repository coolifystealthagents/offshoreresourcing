## Key stats

- Quality metrics need a defined denominator and review sample.
- Speed without accuracy can increase total handling time.
- Escalation quality is measurable when rules and required context are explicit.

## Key takeaways

- Start with three to five measures tied to customer or process outcomes.
- Review a sample of completed work, not activity totals alone.
- Pair every threshold with an owner and response.

## Suggested scorecard

| Measure | Definition | Review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Correct outputs divided by sampled outputs | Weekly sample |
| On-time completion | Items completed inside the agreed window | Weekly queue |
| Rework | Items returned for preventable correction | Root-cause review |
| Escalation quality | Escalations containing required context | Manager sample |
