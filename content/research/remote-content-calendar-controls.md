## Key stats

- Calendar entries are useful only when they have an owner and acceptance criteria.
- Dependency visibility reduces avoidable idle time between research and review.
- A small buffer protects cadence when approvals take longer than planned.

## Key takeaways

- Track status, owner, due date, and next action in every entry.
- Reserve review capacity before filling the writing queue.
- Replan openly when evidence or approval is missing.

## Calendar fields

| Field | Example decision |
| --- | --- |
| Intent | Informational comparison |
| Owner | Research lead |
| Gate | Sources and outline approved |
| Due date | UTC date with buffer |
| Next action | Reviewer checks claims |
