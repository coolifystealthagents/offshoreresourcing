## Key stats

- Capacity includes review, correction, coordination, and maintenance time.
- Concurrent work hides queue growth when unfinished work is not counted.
- A small WIP limit improves visibility of real throughput.

## Key takeaways

- Separate planned hours from available hours.
- Assign capacity to quality gates, not writing alone.
- Reforecast when source collection or approvals expand scope.

## Capacity model

| Lane | Planning question |
| --- | --- |
| Research | How many defensible briefs can be prepared? |
| Drafting | How many complete drafts fit the window? |
| Review | How many claims and links can be checked? |
| Maintenance | What existing content needs attention? |

The [quality scorecard](/research/offshore-team-quality-scorecard) measures output; the [calendar controls](/research/remote-content-calendar-controls) turn capacity into dates.
