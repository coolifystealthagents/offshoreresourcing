## Key stats

- Method notes should make scope, source selection, date, and limitations visible.
- A recommendation is more useful when readers can see which assumption supports it.
- Reproducibility begins with a stable record of inputs and transformations.

## Key takeaways

- State what the article does not measure.
- Explain why sources were included or excluded.
- Separate observed evidence from operating guidance.

## Method note template

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | What population or workflow is covered? |
| Inputs | Which sources and dates were used? |
| Method | How were findings compared? |
| Limits | What could change the conclusion? |

The [source library structure](/research/research-source-library-structure) and [brief version control](/research/research-brief-version-control) support this format.
