## Key stats
- Source budgets should follow claim risk and topic complexity.
- Ten sources is a floor for this Research format, not proof of completeness.
- Primary sources deserve priority when a claim is material.
## Key takeaways
- Budget by claim, not by article length.
- Log exclusions and unresolved gaps.
- Stop collection when evidence is sufficient and diverse.
## Budget method
Use the [content brief research queue](/research/content-brief-research-queue) to list claims, source types, owner, and stop condition.
| Claim risk | Source plan |
| --- | --- |
| Low | One authoritative source plus context |
| Medium | Multiple independent sources |
| High | Primary evidence and specialist review |
## Methodology and limitations
This method applies public research, statistics, search, quality, privacy, and accessibility guidance. It does not estimate labor cost or guarantee claim accuracy.

## FAQs
### When should research stop?
Stop when the central claims are supported, competing interpretations are considered, and remaining gaps are documented.
### Is source diversity always necessary?
For important claims, use sources with genuinely different methods or perspectives where appropriate.
