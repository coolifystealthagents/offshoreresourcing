## Key stats

- Handoff risk rises when the next action, owner, or acceptance evidence is missing.
- A handoff is complete only when the receiving role can act without guessing.
- Control descriptions should identify ownership, evidence, and exception handling.

## Key takeaways

- Use a structured packet rather than a chat-only transfer.
- Mark blockers separately from suggestions.
- Confirm receipt and acceptance with timestamps.

## Risk register

| Risk | Early signal | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing context | Repeated questions | Brief template |
| Lost evidence | Unlinked claims | Source register |
| Ambiguous owner | No due date | RACI-style assignment |
| Time-zone delay | Queue aging | Overlap window |
